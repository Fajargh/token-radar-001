Pre-token protocols, ranked by implied value

Every protocol in the DefiLlama snapshot that records no token, ranked by what its launch would be worth if the market paid it what it pays traded protocols of its size. The price comes from a curve fitted to the traded tokens themselves — market cap against TVL, in logs — and the curve is backtested out of sample, so the board states how far its own predictions usually miss. The watchlist is the floor — every protocol with at least $50M of TVL and no token on record — and the early radar adds every smaller pre-token protocol that passes the same exclusion rule, plus the twenty above the floor the watchlist leaves out, flagged as such. Implied value is a model, not a promise, and never a prediction that a token will be issued.

How the ranking works

Implied value = curve value × growth × maturity × breadth. The curve value is what the fitted size curve predicts for the protocol’s TVL: log10(market cap) = … + … × log10(TVL), fitted by least squares on … traded tokens, R² = …. A protocol twice as big is not worth twice as much: the slope is below one, so the curve pays less per TVL dollar as TVL grows. Growth is the 7-day TVL change clamped to [−50%, +100%] at half weight. Maturity rises with how long the protocol has run with no token, from 0.60 at launch to 1.00 at three years. Breadth adds 2% per extra chain, at most +10%. The same function scores every row; no name is ever special-cased.

The curve and its comparables

The comparables are the traded tokens the curve may learn from: live-token protocols with a positive market cap and TVL, a category, and no excluded shape — no bridge, canonical bridge, liquid-staking, liquid-restaking, restaked-BTC, CEX or chain product; no version marker in the name; no whole word wrapped, staked or staking; not named after an exchange. That is … protocols. A comp is kept when its market-cap-to-TVL ratio lies within one decade of the comps’ median ratio (…×, so the band is …): … are kept and … are set aside as outliers and named below. The band is a mechanical rule from the data’s own median, not a hand-picked range: a parent token’s market cap carried on a sub-protocol, or pass-through staked assets counted as TVL, puts a multiple orders of magnitude from the pack, and such a comp is not comparable.

The backtest: how far the curve usually misses

Every number below is computed from the snapshot at run time, by refitting the band and the curve from scratch for each held-out protocol. The median factor is the typical miss: a leave-one-out median of ×2.67 means half the held-out tokens are predicted within 2.67× of their actual market cap, and half are further off. The curve is an estimate with a stated error, not a precise price.

Who is on the board, and who is not

On the board: the 40 watchlist protocols, the 138 pre-token protocols below the $50M floor that pass the exclusion rule, and 20 protocols above the floor that pass every check the snapshot can show yet are absent from the house watchlist. The house’s rule also uses DefiLlama’s parent-protocol field, which the snapshot does not carry, so the visible record can neither confirm nor deny a parent for those twenty; they are ranked on the visible record and flagged off-watchlist, above floor on their rows, so a reader can weigh them. The field guide says a protocol the list leaves out is no worse a subject than one on it.

Read it honestly

A protocol “records no token” when DefiLlama’s record carries none for it; that is not proof it has none. An implied value is the entry’s own claim about what a launch would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is — never a price, never a promise that a token is coming, and never investment advice. The curve’s backtest says its typical prediction is off by a factor of about two and a half, so treat every implied value as an order of magnitude, not a quote. Farming costs gas and carries smart-contract risk; never pay to claim an airdrop, and claim only from the link the protocol itself publishes.
