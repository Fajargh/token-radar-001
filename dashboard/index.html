Pre-token protocols, ranked by implied value

Every protocol in the DefiLlama snapshot that records no token, ranked by what its launch would be worth if the market paid it what it pays live protocols of the same kind. The watchlist is the floor — every protocol with at least $50M of TVL and no token on record — and the early radar adds every smaller pre-token protocol that passes the same exclusion rule. Implied value is a model, not a promise, and never a prediction that a token will be issued.

How the ranking works

Implied value = TVL × comps multiple × growth × maturity × breadth. The multiple is what the market pays per dollar of TVL for this kind of protocol: the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live-token protocols in the same category, winsorised to [0.02, 1.00] and shrunk toward the global median … with k = 4, so one outlier comp cannot set a category’s price; a category with no live comps takes the global median. Growth is the 7-day TVL change clamped to [−50%, +100%] at half weight. Maturity rises with how long the protocol has run with no token, from 0.60 at launch to 1.00 at three years. Breadth adds 2% per extra chain, at most +10%. The same function scores every row; no name is ever special-cased. The table below is computed from the snapshot at run time.

Who is on the board, and who is not

On the board: the 40 watchlist protocols, plus every pre-token protocol below the $50M floor that passes the exclusion rule in full — not a bridge, canonical bridge, liquid-staking, liquid-restaking, restaked-BTC or CEX product, no version marker in the name, no whole word wrapped, staked or staking, and not named after an exchange. Twenty protocols above the floor pass the visible checks yet are absent from the house watchlist: DefiLlama records a parent protocol for them, a field the snapshot does not carry, so they are products of a protocol whose token already trades — the board does not rank them as launches, and names them here instead of hiding them.

Read it honestly

A protocol “records no token” when DefiLlama’s record carries none for it; that is not proof it has none. An implied value is the entry’s own claim about what a launch would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is — never a price, never a promise that a token is coming, and never investment advice. Farming costs gas and carries smart-contract risk; never pay to claim an airdrop, and claim only from the link the protocol itself publishes.
